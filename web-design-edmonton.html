What we plan
into your website.

Designed for phones and desktops

Customers should be able to read your services, find your phone number and navigate comfortably on the device they are using.

Clear service and contact pages

Explain your offer in plain language. Use relevant photos and portfolio examples, and give visitors a clear route to a call, email or quote request.

Search-friendly structure

Descriptive titles, logical headings, useful service content, internal links and a sitemap help search engines discover and understand the site. For local businesses, describe your actual service area without inventing office locations.

Website project questions

What will my website cost?

Every quote depends on scope. Page count, content, functionality and integrations all affect cost. We will explain the project price and any separate ongoing charges before you commit.

Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No. A well-structured website is a foundation for search visibility, but rankings depend on many factors and take time. We focus on useful content and sound technical work.

Can we work together outside Edmonton?

Yes. Website and branding projects can be handled remotely across Alberta and Canada. Any on-location production work needs separate planning.
