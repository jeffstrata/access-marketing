Before we get started

Good questions.
Straight answers.

How much does a business website cost?

Pricing depends on the pages, content, functionality and support you need. Tell us about your business and we will provide a written quote. Hosting, domain costs and ongoing maintenance should be agreed separately in your project scope.

Do you work with businesses outside Edmonton?

Yes. We are based in Edmonton and serve businesses throughout Alberta and across Canada through remote collaboration.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share your current website and what you would like to improve. We can discuss a redesign, clearer service content, mobile usability and search-friendly page structure.

Will my website appear first on Google?

No one can guarantee a first-place ranking. We can build useful service content, descriptive page titles, crawlable links and a sound technical foundation. Search visibility also depends on competition, your reputation and ongoing work.

How long does a website take?

The timeline depends on scope and how quickly content and feedback are available. We will agree on milestones before starting your project.
